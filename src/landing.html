The documented problem

The information exists.
Students just cannot find it.

A survey of 21 international students at Dunaújváros Egyetem identified clear, systematic failures in information access — with real consequences for student wellbeing and academic performance.

52%
Language barrier as primary confusion source
Over half of respondents cited Hungarian-only forms, official letters, and Neptun messages as their biggest source of confusion on arrival.
43%
Could not find the right office
Students did not know which office handled which problem — and were frequently redirected between departments without a resolution.
38%
Banking and administrative failures
"The international office had no solution." Students resolved critical administrative problems through peer networks, not institutional support.
System architecture

Retrieval-Augmented Generation
built for this context.

Not a generic chatbot. A university-specific information engine built on BM25 retrieval over a curated knowledge base of institutional documents, powered by Llama 3 via the Groq API.

Step 01
Student query
Any question in English or Hungarian — about Neptun, deadlines, scholarships, visas, fees, or any administrative process.
Step 02
Office routing
A keyword classifier detects which of ten university offices is relevant and filters the knowledge base accordingly before retrieval begins.
Step 03
BM25 retrieval
The top matching document chunks are retrieved in milliseconds. No embeddings, no GPU, no external inference cost — fully deployable on free-tier infrastructure.
Step 04
Grounded answer
Llama 3 generates a precise, source-attributed response locked to the student's chosen language. The model cannot switch languages regardless of query content.
What is built

Three portals.
One integrated system.

UniAdvisor is not just a chat interface — it is a full platform with distinct roles for students, academic staff, and administrators.

Student portal
Conversational AI advisor
24/7 access to accurate, sourced answers about university procedures without requiring Hungarian language proficiency.
  • English and Hungarian language lock
  • 10 office categories auto-detected
  • Conversation history and follow-up suggestions
  • Escalation to human advisor
  • Thumbs up/down feedback on every response
Admin command centre
Analytics and oversight
Real-time visibility into student information needs, satisfaction scores, escalation management, and document knowledge base.
  • Per-office question volume and ratings
  • Survey results and research data dashboard
  • Document upload and knowledge base management
  • Full audit log of all staff actions
Staff portal
Communication tools
Publishing interface for announcements, live student activity monitoring, and direct student messaging.
  • Scheduled and urgent announcements
  • Live activity feed
  • Student directory with nationality data
International focus
Built for the underserved cohort
Every feature is designed for students arriving without Hungarian language skills or prior institutional knowledge — the group most affected by information failures.
  • Visa and residence permit guidance
  • Stipendium Hungaricum information
  • Neptun step-by-step guide in English
  • Pre-arrival checklist
